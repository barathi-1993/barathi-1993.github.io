This project developed methods for identifying abnormal fabric-product images and localizing defects for automated visual quality control.

**Research period:** September 2022 to February 2024  
**Funder:** National Research Foundation (NRF), South Korea  
**Role:** Project Lead

## Objectives

- Separate defective and non-defective fabric images.
- Detect and localize abnormal regions to support image-based industrial inspection.

## Contributions

- Applied spectral clustering to group images for defect/non-defect binary classification.
- Used transfer learning with deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for defect detection and localization.
- Developed computer vision experiments for assessing image-based product quality.

## Methods

Spectral clustering, anomaly detection, image classification, convolutional neural networks, transfer learning, defect localization, industrial visual inspection.
