My Stanford research has included end-to-end histopathology pipelines from slide preparation through downstream analysis.

## Focus

- WSI and tile extraction, artifact filtering, and quality control.
- Foundation-model feature extraction and multimodal image/text analysis.
- QuPath-guided annotation, ground-truth quality assurance, tissue classification and segmentation.
- Reproducible dataset construction and model evaluation.

**Role:** Postdoctoral researcher. Specific datasets, numerical metrics, and clinical claims are documented in the associated research outputs rather than inferred here.
