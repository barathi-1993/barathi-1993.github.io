Gesture2Music is a real-time, gesture-driven music generation system that maps continuous human movement into musical outputs. It connects visual landmark tracking and temporal sequence models with interactive music generation.

**Role:** Collaborating researcher  
**Research area:** Human–computer interaction, gesture recognition, temporal learning

## Research contributions

- Developed gesture-recognition workflows using MediaPipe-based face and hand landmarks.
- Built temporal modeling pipelines for learning patterns in continuous gesture sequences.
- Contributed to low-latency inference and continuous gesture-to-music interaction.
- Supported system evaluation and integration of computer vision with generated musical output.
