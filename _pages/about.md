## About

I am a computer vision researcher with postdoctoral research experience in Stanford University's Department of Pathology. My work bridges algorithm development and domain-focused application, from large-scale histopathology datasets to interactive, low-latency vision systems.

At Stanford, my research has involved whole-slide image preprocessing, foundation-model features, multimodal pathology analysis, annotation and dataset curation, and patient-level prediction. Earlier research at Kyungpook National University included gesture recognition, industrial anomaly detection, small-object detection, and optical character recognition.

I earned a Ph.D. in Computer Vision from Kyungpook National University in 2024. My research interests include reliable computer vision, multimodal learning, healthcare applications and the design of usable AI systems.
