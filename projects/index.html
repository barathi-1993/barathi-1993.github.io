Medical AI · Computational Pathology

Multimodal Analysis in Digital Pathology

Jul 2024 to Present
Funder: National Institutes of Health (NIH), USA
Role: Postdoctoral Researcher

Developing end-to-end whole-slide image (WSI) and multimodal histopathology pipelines, with an emphasis on scalable image processing, trustworthy data quality, pathology foundation models, and reproducible downstream analysis.

Research objectives & contributions
  • Build workflows for WSI and tile-level tissue classification, tumor segmentation, and image-text analysis.
  • Design artifact filtering, tile extraction, and image-quality checks for consistent model inputs.
  • Use QuPath for tumor-region annotations and reference-label quality assurance.
  • Curate large-scale, tile-level histopathology datasets for benchmarking and reproducible evaluation.
  • Train and integrate image and multimodal foundation models for biomedical analysis and reporting.
  • In related clinical laboratory AI work, develop automated analysis of serum protein electrophoresis and immunofixation electrophoresis, including monoclonal peak quantification and abnormal-band detection.

Industrial Computer Vision

Abnormal Image Detection and Localization of Fabric Products

Sep 2022 to Feb 2024
Funder: National Research Foundation (NRF), South Korea
Role: Project Lead

Developed image-analysis methods to distinguish defective from non-defective fabric products and localize irregularities for automated quality-control inspection.

Research objectives & contributions
  • Apply spectral clustering to separate defect and non-defect image groups.
  • Use transfer learning with deep convolutional neural networks for defect detection and localization.
  • Develop repeatable vision-based workflows for industrial anomaly inspection.

Gesture Recognition · Human-Computer Interaction

Gesture Recognition and Interactive Systems

Feb 2022 to May 2023
Funder: School of Computer Science and Engineering, Kyungpook National University, South Korea
Role: Project Lead

Investigated real-time gesture understanding for Indian Sign Language and interactive applications through visual landmark tracking and temporal sequence learning.

Research objectives & contributions
  • Curate multi-label datasets for sign-language recognition and gesture-driven music interaction.
  • Extract timestamped face, hand, and pose landmark sequences using MediaPipe.
  • Train GRU-based models to learn the temporal dynamics of human gestures.
  • Map recognized gestures into language or musical representations for interactive output.

Efficient Object Detection

Lightweight Model for Disabled Parking Sign Detection

Jul 2021 to Dec 2021
Funder: Small and Medium Business Technology Information Promotion Agency, South Korea
Role: Project Developer and Lead

Developed a lightweight, feature-fusion-based object detector to improve small disabled-parking-sign localization while controlling model size and computational cost.

Research objectives & contributions
  • Enhance YOLOv5 for small-object detection through PANet and weighted BiFPN feature fusion.
  • Explore architectural simplifications to reduce parameters and floating-point operations.
  • Evaluate on 1,025 original sign images expanded through augmentation to approximately 15,000 samples.

Affective Computing · Intelligent Systems

Real-Time Emotion Recognition System

Mar 2021 to Feb 2022
Funder: Small and Medium Business Technology Information Promotion Agency, South Korea
Role: Project Developer

Investigated webcam-based expression analysis and an emotion-aware decision-support prototype using multi-region landmark extraction and machine-learning models.

Research objectives & contributions
  • Curate a custom webcam dataset with diverse facial expressions and recording conditions.
  • Extract face, hand, and body landmarks with MediaPipe.
  • Train and benchmark multiple machine-learning approaches for real-time emotion recognition.
  • Explore integration with a digital-twin prototype; this research is not presented as a clinically validated diagnostic system.
